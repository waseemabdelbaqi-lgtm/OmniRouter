# How to Use This Book

Book Three contains the complete solutions for Book One and Book Two of this collection. It is organized in three parts that match the question books exactly.

:::box objectives
- **Part I** — worked solutions to every classroom example in Book One, organized by unit.
- **Part II** — answers and explanations for every classified question in Book Two: MCQ first, then FRQ, for each unit.
- **Part III** — complete solutions to the four predicted examinations.
:::

Solutions to the College Board released questions in Book Four are printed at the end of Book Four, not here.

### Getting the most from a solution

- Attempt the question first. Look only at the answer line to check your result, then read the explanation to confirm your reasoning.
- For calculations, check the formula, the substitution, the units, and the significant figures, not only the final number.
- For free-response questions, compare each part with the scoring idea shown in italics. A point is earned for the specific idea, so be explicit in your own answers.
- Each solution lists its question ID. IDs beginning **SG** are Book One classroom examples, **QB** are classified Book Two questions, and **PE** are predicted-exam questions.
