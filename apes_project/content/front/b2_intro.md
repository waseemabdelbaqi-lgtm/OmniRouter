# How to Use This Book

This book contains original AP Environmental Science practice material written by Success4Sure Academy. It has two parts: nine unit-by-unit question banks and four complete predicted examinations.

:::box objectives
- Practice every unit with multiple-choice questions (MCQ) first and free-response questions (FRQ) second.
- Build exam stamina with four full-length predicted exams that follow the 2026 exam format.
- Check your work in Book Three, which contains a complete solution for every question here.
:::

### The 2026 exam format

| Section | Questions | Weight | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Multiple choice | 80 (four choices, A–D) | 60% | 90 minutes |
| II — Free response | 3 (10 points each) | 40% | 70 minutes |

The three free-response questions follow a fixed pattern: **FRQ 1** asks you to design an investigation, **FRQ 2** asks you to analyze and interpret quantitative data, and **FRQ 3** asks you to analyze an environmental problem by doing calculations. A calculator is allowed on both sections.

### Suggested study plan

1. Finish a unit in Book One, then do that unit's MCQ section without notes.
2. Mark every question you miss, find the matching topic number (printed in the solution), and re-read that topic in Book One.
3. Write full responses to the FRQs on separate paper before you read the solutions.
4. Take each predicted exam under timed conditions. Take Exam 1 early to find weak units, and Exam 4 close to test day.

### Task verbs in free-response questions

| Verb | What it asks for |
|---|---|
| Calculate | Show the formula, the substitution with units, and the final answer with units |
| Describe | Give the relevant characteristics of the topic |
| Explain | Say how or why, using evidence or reasoning |
| Identify | State the answer without elaboration |
| Justify | Support a claim with evidence and the reasoning that connects it |
| Make a claim | State a position based on evidence or knowledge |
| Propose a solution | Offer a solution supported by evidence or knowledge |

Answers and explanations are **not** included in this book. See Book Three, Parts II and III.
