# About This Book

This book organizes questions from College Board released AP Environmental Science materials by the nine units of the 2026 course. It is a study tool built from three things: a verified year for every question, an original task description, and an original, complete solution.

Use the official released exams and free-response materials published by the College Board (apcentral.collegeboard.org) with this book.

### How the book is organized

- **Part I — Classified Questions.** Nine units. Inside every unit, multiple-choice (MCQ) entries come first and free-response (FRQ) entries second. Each entry shows its verified examination year.
- **Part II — Complete Solutions.** All solutions are printed at the end of the book, by unit, MCQ first and FRQ second, in the same order as the questions.

### Notes on older exams

Exams before 2020 used five answer choices (A–E) in the multiple-choice section; the 2026 exam uses four (A–D). Where a question is based on subject matter that is no longer part of the 2026 course, the item has been left out of this book. Free-response questions are classified by their primary assessed concept, with secondary units recorded internally.
