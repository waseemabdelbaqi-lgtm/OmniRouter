## Chapter 11 — Stratospheric Ozone and Global Warming

:::sol SG-C11-E001

(a) Rise = height × expansion per °C × temperature change = 1,000 m × (2 × 10⁻⁴ per °C) × 0.5°C = 1,000 × 0.0001 = 0.1 m = 10 cm. (b) The water expands as it warms without any added water, so this is thermal expansion. Melting land ice adds new water and also raises sea level.

:::sol SG-C11-E004
**Answer: (a) 2,400 kg before and 720 kg after; (b) 70% reduction; (c) mitigation; a cleaner grid.**
(a) Gasoline car: 12,000 km × 0.20 kg/km = 2,400 kg CO₂. Electric car: electricity = 12,000 km × 0.15 kWh/km = 1,800 kWh; CO₂ = 1,800 kWh × 0.40 kg/kWh = 720 kg. (b) Percent reduction = (2,400 − 720) ÷ 2,400 × 100 = 1,680 ÷ 2,400 × 100 = 70%. (c) It lowers the cause of warming, so it is mitigation. Charging from wind, solar or other low-carbon electricity (a lower kg CO₂ per kWh) would cut the 720 kg further.
:::

**Answer: (a) about 2,100 tigers; (b) about 18 years (17.5); (c) see below.**
(a) Each year the population is multiplied by 1 − 0.04 = 0.96. After 10 years: 3,200 × 0.96¹⁰ = 3,200 × 0.6648 = 2,127, which is 2,100 to the nearest 100. (b) Rule of 70: halving time = 70 ÷ 4 = 17.5 years, about 18 years. (c) Tigers are K-selected: they are large, mature slowly and have few young per litter, so populations recover slowly. Strong anti-poaching laws with enforcement (or protecting their habitat) target poaching.
