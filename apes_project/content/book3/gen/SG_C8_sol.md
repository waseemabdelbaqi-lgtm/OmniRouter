:::sol SG-U6-E020
**Answer:** model design below (any logical equivalent earns credit).

(a) Hypothesis: *If a wind farm is built, then the noise level (dB) at nearby homes will be higher than before it was built (and highest at the homes closest to the turbines).* Independent variable: the presence of the wind farm (before versus after), or distance from the turbines. Dependent variable: sound level measured in decibels (dB) at the homes.

(b) Design: Measure noise at several homes near the planned site before construction (baseline), at the same times of day and under similar weather and wind conditions. Choose a control location of similar land use and distance from roads that is far from the turbines and measure it on the same schedule. After the wind farm starts operating, measure again at the same homes and the control site using the same meter, height, time of day and season. Take replicate readings (for example, three at each location, on several days) and record wind speed as a controlled variable. Other acceptable variables: distance from the turbines, or turbine operating versus shut down.

(c) Compare the mean change after construction with the spread (range or standard deviation) of the baseline readings. If the average increase is larger than the natural variation in the baseline, and the control site shows no similar rise, the increase is attributed to the wind farm. Graph readings versus distance; noise typically falls with distance and levels off at the background value. Use averages from replicates, not single readings, and state the limits of the data.
