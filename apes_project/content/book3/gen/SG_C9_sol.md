**Answer:** model design below; a downwind rise alone does not prove the quarry is the cause.

(a) *If the quarry raises PM10, then PM10 will be higher at the monitor 1 km east (downwind) than at a monitor upwind of the quarry.*

(b) Independent variable: monitor location (downwind of the quarry versus upwind/background site). Dependent variable: PM10 concentration (µg/m³). (a) Generated = 80,000 people × 1.6 kg/person/day = 128,000 kg/day = 128 t/day. The landfill receives 100% − 20% − 10% = 70%, so 128 t/day × 0.70 = 89.6 t/day. Annual mass = 89.6 t/day × 365 days = 32,704 t, or **about 3.27 × 10⁴ t per year**.

(b) Volume = mass ÷ density = 32,704 t × 1,000 kg/t ÷ 550 kg/m³ = 3.2704 × 10⁷ kg ÷ 550 kg/m³ = 59,462 m³, or **about 5.9 × 10⁴ m³ per year**.

(c) Years = 2.0 × 10⁶ m³ ÷ 59,462 m³/yr = 33.6, or **about 34 years**.

(d) Energy in the methane = 4.0 × 10⁵ m³ × 36 MJ/m³ = 1.44 × 10⁷ MJ. Electrical energy = 1.44 × 10⁷ MJ × 0.35 = 5.04 × 10⁶ MJ. Convert using 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ: 5.04 × 10⁶ ÷ 3.6 = 1.4 × 10⁶ kWh, so **1.4 × 10⁶ kWh per year**.
