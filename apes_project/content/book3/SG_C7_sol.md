## Chapter 7 — Land and Water Use

:::sol SG-C7-E001
**Answer: (a) 803,000 kcal per person per year. (b) 3.212 × 10¹³ kcal per year. (c) about 6.4 × 10⁶ hectares (6.42 million ha).**

(a) Energy per person = 2,200 kcal/day × 365 days = **803,000 kcal/year**.

(b) Total = 803,000 kcal/person/yr × 40,000,000 people = **3.212 × 10¹³ kcal/yr**.

(c) Hectares = 3.212 × 10¹³ kcal/yr ÷ 5.0 × 10⁶ kcal/ha/yr = 6.424 × 10⁶ ha, or about **6.4 million hectares**.
:::

:::sol SG-C7-E003
**Answer: (a) 600 t per year. (b) 120 t per year under no-till; 480 t saved. (c) 15 times.**

(a) 15 t/ha × 40 ha = **600 t/yr**.

(b) No-till loss per hectare = 15 × (1 − 0.80) = 3 t/ha; × 40 ha = **120 t/yr**. Saved = 600 − 120 = **480 t/yr**.

(c) 15 t/ha/yr ÷ 1 t/ha/yr = **15 times** the formation rate, so the soil is being lost much faster than it forms.
:::

:::sol SG-C7-E004
**Answer: (a) 3,000 m³ per year. (b) 40% over. (c) e.g., selective cutting (drawback: higher cost per cubic meter and more skill needed).**

(a) Sustainable harvest = 6 m³/ha/yr × 500 ha = **3,000 m³/yr**.

(b) Percent over = (4,200 − 3,000) ÷ 3,000 × 100% = 1,200 ÷ 3,000 × 100% = **40%**.

(c) Selective cutting (or strip cutting with reseeding, or certified harvest limited to growth) keeps canopy, soil and habitat while producing wood. Drawback: costs more per cubic meter, needs skilled workers, and yields less volume at once.
:::

:::sol SG-C7-E008
**Answer: (a) 12.96 km². (b) 11.52 km². (c) 11.1% decrease; largest fragment interior 5.76 km².**

(a) Interior is a square of side 4 − 2(0.2) = 3.6 km. Area = 3.6² = **12.96 km²**.

(b) Each 2 km × 4 km fragment has interior (2 − 0.4) × (4 − 0.4) = 1.6 × 3.6 = 5.76 km². Two fragments: **11.52 km²**.

(c) Decrease = (12.96 − 11.52) ÷ 12.96 × 100% = 1.44 ÷ 12.96 × 100% = **11.1%**. The largest single interior fragment is **5.76 km²**, less than half the original interior.
:::

:::sol SG-C7-E010
**Answer: (a) 40 years. (b) 2.25 million t per year; about 53 years. (c) Higher price makes lower-grade or harder deposits profitable.**

(a) Years = 120 million t ÷ 3.0 million t/yr = **40 years**.

(b) New production = 3.0 × (1 − 0.25) = **2.25 million t/yr**. Years = 120 ÷ 2.25 = 53.3, about **53 years**.

(c) A reserve is the part of the resource that can be mined profitably. If the price rises, deposits already known but too expensive to mine become profitable, so they move into reserves.

:::sol SG-C7-E011
**Answer: (a) $102 per tonne. (b) $11,000,000 per year. (c) 27.5% increase; higher price tends to reduce use or encourage efficiency.**

(a) Full-cost price = $80 + $22 = **$102/t**.

(b) Revenue = $22/t × 500,000 t = **$11,000,000/yr**.

(c) Percent increase = 22 ÷ 80 × 100% = **27.5%**. A higher price gives buyers and producers a reason to use less of the product and the water and soil it damages, or to adopt cleaner methods.
:::
