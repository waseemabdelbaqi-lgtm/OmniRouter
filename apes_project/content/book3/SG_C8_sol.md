## Chapter 8 — Energy

:::sol SG-C8-E001
**Answer: (a) 270 kWh; (b) 972 MJ, $37.80; (c) about 771 kWh of fuel energy.**

(a) Energy = power × time = 1.5 kW × 6.0 h/day × 30 days = 270 kWh.

(b) 270 kWh × 3.6 MJ/kWh = 972 MJ. Cost = 270 kWh × $0.14/kWh = $37.80.

(c) Fuel energy = electricity ÷ efficiency = 270 kWh ÷ 0.35 = 771 kWh (the rest, about 501 kWh, is waste heat).
:::

:::sol SG-C8-E002
**Answer: (a) 40% in 1900 and 26.7% in 2020; (b) coal +700%, total +1,100%; (c) the total grew faster than coal.**

(a) 1900: 20 ÷ 50 × 100 = 40%. 2020: 160 ÷ 600 × 100 = 26.7%.

(b) Coal: (160 − 20) ÷ 20 × 100 = 700%. Total: (600 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 1,100%.

(c) Coal use rose eightfold but total energy use rose twelvefold, because oil, gas and other sources grew even faster. Coal's share is its amount divided by the total, so it can fall while its amount rises.
:::

:::sol SG-C8-E003
**Answer: (a) 750 gal and 500 gal; (b) 2,225 kg CO₂; (c) 2,025 kg CO₂; (d) about 0.99 kg CO₂/kWh.**

(a) Fuel = distance ÷ mpg. Car A: 15,000 ÷ 20 = 750 gal. Car B: 15,000 ÷ 30 = 500 gal.

(b) Gallons saved = 750 − 500 = 250 gal; 250 × 8.9 kg/gal = 2,225 kg CO₂.

(c) Electricity = 0.30 kWh/mi × 15,000 mi = 4,500 kWh; 4,500 × 0.45 kg/kWh = 2,025 kg CO₂.

(d) Car B emits 8.9 ÷ 30 = 0.2967 kg CO₂ per mile. Set 0.30 kWh/mi × f = 0.2967 kg/mi, so f = 0.2967 ÷ 0.30 = 0.989 ≈ 0.99 kg CO₂/kWh.
:::

:::sol SG-C8-E004
**Answer: (a) bus 0.060 kg, car 0.16 kg per passenger-km; (b) about 2.7 times; (c) 1,250 kg CO₂ saved; (d) 8 riders.**

(a) Bus: 1.2 ÷ 20 = 0.060 kg CO₂ per passenger-km. Car: 0.20 ÷ 1.25 = 0.16 kg CO₂ per passenger-km.

(b) 0.16 ÷ 0.060 = 2.67 ≈ 2.7 times.

(c) Distance = 25 km × 2 × 250 = 12,500 km. Car: 12,500 × 0.16 = 2,000 kg. Bus: 12,500 × 0.060 = 750 kg. Saved = 2,000 − 750 = 1,250 kg CO₂.

(d) Need 1.2 ÷ n ≤ 0.16, so n ≥ 7.5. The minimum whole number is 8 riders.
:::
