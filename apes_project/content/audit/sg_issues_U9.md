# SG issues — Unit 9

## SG-U9-E015(c)
"Calculate the target and the average yearly cut needed from its current level after the project." The project already reduces emissions from 20,000 to 12,425 t CO2e/yr (7,575 t saved), which is below the 25%-reduction target of 15,000 t. The needed yearly cut is therefore negative (target already met by 2,575 t). Solution states this; also gives 625 t/yr if "current level" were read as the original 20,000. Suggested fix: use a larger pledge (e.g. 50% below original = 10,000 t, cut of (12,425 - 10,000)/8 = 303 t/yr) or a smaller project.
