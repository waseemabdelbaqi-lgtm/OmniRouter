# Unit 7 — Atmospheric Pollution

:::box note
Exam weight: Unit 7 accounts for about 7–10% of the AP Environmental Science exam score.
This section assesses air pollutant sources and effects, photochemical smog, thermal inversion, indoor air pollutants, pollution controls, acid deposition, and noise.
Expect data and graph interpretation, short calculations, and experimental-design judgments, so show units and reason from evidence.
:::
