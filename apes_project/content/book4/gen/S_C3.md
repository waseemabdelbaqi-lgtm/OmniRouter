:::sol CB-C3-MCQ001 | label=Question 1 | year=2008
**Official key: D.** Freshwater availability per person is lowest in arid regions with large populations and little renewable runoff. The keyed location is the Middle East/Arabian Peninsula, which gets very little rainfall and has few permanent rivers. The other marked locations are humid or river-rich regions with far more water per person.

:::sol CB-C3-MCQ002 | label=Question 2 | year=2008
**Official key: D.** Desalination removes salt from seawater and needs large energy inputs, so it is used mainly where freshwater is extremely scarce and energy is cheap or plentiful. The Persian Gulf/Middle East region (point D) fits this, with oil-funded desalination plants supplying most municipal water. Other regions have enough rivers or groundwater.
:::
