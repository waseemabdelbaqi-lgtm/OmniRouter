**(b)(ii)** *(1 pt)* The surplus is used up and the population overshoots carrying capacity and crashes from starvation and competition; the open, hotter ground may also exceed their temperature tolerance.

**(c)** *(change + effect)* Removing trees lowers photosynthetic uptake and destroys a carbon sink; burning or decay of the wood releases CO₂; machinery burns fossil fuel; or lost shade and evapotranspiration raise local temperatures. Link the change to more greenhouse gas or more warming.
