:::sol CB-C11-FRQ001 | label=Question 1 | year=2013
(a) Stratospheric ozone absorbs ultraviolet (mainly UV-B and UV-C) radiation; the health benefit is lower rates of skin cancer, sunburn or cataracts. *(1 + 1.)*

(b) UV splits ozone: O₃ + UV → O₂ + O. The free oxygen atom meets an O₂ molecule: O + O₂ → O₃. The two steps continuously recycle ozone and absorb UV. *(1 point for each process.)*

(c)(i) UV breaks chlorine atoms off CFC molecules (e.g., CCl₃F + UV → CCl₂F + Cl); each free Cl then attacks ozone: Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂, and the chlorine is regenerated so one atom destroys many ozone molecules. *(1 + 1.)*
(ii) Ozone recovery lags because CFCs are very long-lived and take years to reach the stratosphere, chlorine already present stays aloft for decades, and other ozone-depleting substances are still emitted. *(1 point for one.)*

(d) Burning fossil fuels (e.g., vehicle exhaust) emits NOₓ and VOCs, which react to form ozone. Ozone peaks in the daytime because the reactions are photochemical and need sunlight. *(1 + 1.)*

(e) Ecological: ozone damages plant tissue and reduces photosynthesis/crop growth. Health: ozone irritates the respiratory system and aggravates asthma. *(1 + 1.)*
:::
