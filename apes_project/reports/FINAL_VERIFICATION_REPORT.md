## What was verified (and how)
- **Frame and watermark:** one identical double maroon frame (#7A1424, 20 pt inset, corner ornaments) and one identical large logo watermark (24.6 cm, top-left → bottom-right, 13% opacity, behind text) are present on all 801 pages (automated per-page check of the final PDFs + identical XML in all four DOCX + visual inspection of sampled pages from every book).
- **Content integrity:** 0 missing-figure markers, 0 leaked markup, 0 blank pages, all table-of-contents page numbers match their headings (215 of 215), every figure reference placed in the DOCX (63/63 in Book One, 173/173 in Book Two).
- **Links and QR codes:** the three required URLs are clickable on the contact page of every book, and all three QR codes decode to the correct URLs in all four books.
- **Protected PDFs:** AES-256, open without a password, all permission bits cleared (no print / copy / edit).
- **Answer keys:** all 680 MCQ (360 in the unit banks, 320 in the four exams) were solved blind by independent reviewers before seeing the keys. One real key error (PE2-MCQ014, sea-turtle survivorship) was found and fixed; one logging slip (QB-U2-MCQ013) was checked by hand — key D is correct. All FRQ arithmetic and 10-point totals were re-checked; several solution errors were corrected (see scientific_accuracy_report).
- **Question-to-solution matching:** 140/140 Book One examples, 730/730 Book Two questions and 534/534 Book Four items have a matching solution.
- **Study Guide gap audit:** 1,673 question-to-concept checks across Books Two and Four and the four exams; 1,575 fully covered after expansion; 96 flagged as beyond the 2026 curriculum (deliberately not added); 2 deferred to another unit. About 17,500 words, 14 new figures and 46 new unsolved examples were added to Book One. Book One now has 140 unsolved examples; all are solved in Book Three Part I.

## Known limitations and judgement calls (please read)
1. **College Board content:** no republication licence was supplied, so Book Four reproduces **no** College Board question text or figures — each entry is an original description with the verified year, plus an original solution. 2007 and 2009 FRQs (and part of 2008) are absent because pages 281–420 of the FRQ compilation were not uploaded. 2008 is a practice exam that was never administered. The 2024 exam copies had no published key; those MCQ answers were derived by Success4Sure Academy and are labelled so, and 11 low-confidence 2024 items were left out.
2. **College Board links were not verified** (the site was unreachable from the production environment), so no College Board URL is printed; the books name apcentral.collegeboard.org only.
3. **Prep-book sources:** two commercial prep books were used only as topic checklists (one carries a pirate-site watermark); nothing was copied.
4. **Fonts:** DOCX files specify Times New Roman; the PDFs embed Liberation Serif (metric-compatible) because Times New Roman is not installed on the build machine. DOCX files were produced and converted with LibreOffice; they have not been opened in Microsoft Word.
5. **Logo quality:** the company profile stores the logo only as a 150 ppi raster, so the highest-quality version was rebuilt from that raster plus the author's sharper watermark art, scaled to the official proportions. A vector logo file would give a crisper watermark.
6. **Option-length bias:** in the MCQ banks the correct option is still the longest in roughly 40–50% of items (down after rebalancing); a further editing pass would help.
7. **Data in questions and figures is invented** (labelled illustrative); a few reference values (e.g. radon action level) are standard figures from memory and should be spot-checked by the author.
8. **PDF protection is advisory** and does not stop screenshots or OCR.
9. A few pages end with white space where a figure or heading is kept with its text; this is intentional.
10. The Book One gap additions came in a little over some per-unit page budgets, but the book remains inside its 250-page preferred limit.
